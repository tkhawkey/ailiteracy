Engineer the prompt

For each prompt below: (1) diagnose what's missing, (2) rewrite it, (3) predict what the improved output will look like.

Prompt anatomy — five components
Role — Who should the AI act as?
Task — What precise action — what output format?
Context — What does the AI need to know about you?
Format — How should the output be structured?
Constraints — What should it avoid or limit?
