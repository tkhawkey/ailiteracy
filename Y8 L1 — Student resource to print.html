Part 2 — Group scenario
The scenario

A school uses an AI system that has access to every student's essay folder. It flags any student whose writing has declined compared to their previous work — and automatically emails their parents when a student is flagged. Teachers only see the flagged students' work; unflagged work is never reviewed.

1What could go wrong — even if the AI is technically accurate?
2At what point should a human have been involved?
3Who is responsible if the AI's assessment is wrong?
4Would you want this system operating on your work?
Part 3 — What changes the stakes?

Your teacher will introduce four factors that make agentic AI higher stakes. For each one, note how it applies to the essay-flagging scenario — or to any of today's other scenarios.

Reversibility — can the action be undone?
Accountability — is a human clearly responsible?
Transparency — does anyone know what the AI decided and why?
Consent — did the people affected agree?
