AI in the Cambridge Digital Literacy Curriculum

Extracted from 0072 Primary (Stages 1–6) and 0082 Lower Secondary (Stages 7–9)

AI is woven throughout both frameworks rather than siloed into discrete units. Six consistent themes run from Stage 1 through Stage 9, building in complexity and critical demand at each level. Across all stages, the curriculum insists that AI is a tool that supports learning — not a replacement for human thought, relationships, or original understanding.

Prompting & creation — from simple text inputs in Stage 1 to full prompt engineering and framework comparison in Stage 9
Critical evaluation — checking AI output for accuracy, bias, validity, and source trustworthiness at every stage
Learning integrity — strong, recurring thread: AI must not shortcut genuine understanding or owned output
Safety & wellbeing — digital footprints, parasocial AI relationships, toxic AI behaviour, emotional risks
AI and society — jobs, industry disruption, regulation, climate impact, data ownership, digital divides
Human vs AI intelligence — comparing capabilities, limitations, emotional understanding, and co-intelligence
